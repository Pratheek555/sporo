## Healing a tattoo between sea breeze and scooter traffic

White Town is lovely to live in and slightly tricky to heal in. The air is humid most of the year and salty near the sea. The sun is strong almost every day, and the north-east monsoon (roughly October to December) brings heavy downpours. Here's how to handle each of them.

- **Let swimming wait.** Keep the tattoo out of the sea, pools and other soaking water until it is fully healed. The end of peeling does not, by itself, mean the skin is ready for a swim.
- **Cover up on your walks.** Use loose clothing and shade while the skin heals. Put sunscreen on the tattoo only after the skin has fully healed.
- **Plan cleaning after the journey.** Ask how to clean the tattoo after travel or sweating, and follow the routine for your dressing and placement. Use clean hands and avoid rubbing the area.
- **Check product instructions.** Ask which products to use, in what amount and at which stage of healing. Follow that advice instead of adding extra layers because the weather feels humid.
- **Mind the monsoon.** Getting caught in the rain won't ruin a tattoo, but don't leave it wet. Rinse with clean water, pat dry and change out of damp clothes.

Table: A rough healing timeline
| When | What you'll notice | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Dressing still in place | Follow the instructions for the dressing you receive | Ask when and how to remove or change it |
| Cleaning the area | Use clean hands and the recommended routine | Pat dry gently and avoid rubbing with a shared towel |
| Skin still healing | Avoid soaking, scratching and friction | Keep it out of direct sun and follow your aftercare instructions |
| Fully healed skin | Discuss any uncertainty about healing before swimming | Use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher on exposed tattooed skin |

Everyone heals a little differently. If something looks wrong, message us. If redness spreads, you develop a fever, or the pain is getting worse rather than better, see a doctor promptly.

## Aftercare for people who never stop moving

Town-centre life is busy: crowded streets, shopping bags, two-wheelers, dust and plenty of sweat. Puducherry is humid most of the year, the sun is strong, and the north-east monsoon brings heavy rain from roughly October to December. Here's how to heal well through all of it.

- **Watch the bag straps.** Heavy shopping bags and backpack straps rubbing on a fresh shoulder or forearm tattoo can irritate it. Switch sides or carry less for a week.
